How this works

What this is. Every game is graded by what happened next: your result, then how your opponent did across the rest of its season with this game removed, then how their opponents did in turn. Teams are ranked within each day, that finish becomes a 0–100 score, and a team's rating is the average of its days. No preseason poll, no margin of victory, no votes.

Why rankings move backwards. A November win is not settled in November. It gains value if the team you beat keeps winning and loses value if they collapse. Drag the date slider and you are watching the season reinterpret itself. Click any team to see each of its games and how their worth has shifted: the small line beside each game is that game alone, re‑graded after every day since it was played. Hover it for the day‑by‑day figures.

The victors gate. To be listed you must have beaten somebody worth beating, and that team must qualify too — the rule is applied over and over until the set stops changing. It needs enough cross‑conference play to work, so it stays off through the opening fortnight and tightens as the season fills in. It never changes a rating or an order; it only decides who is shown.
